# 0.6.0 — 2026-11-03

## Added

- **`gnomad_get_variant_cooccurrence`**: takes two GRCh37 coordinate IDs and returns `p_compound_heterozygous` and labelled genotype and haplotype counts, overall and per ancestry group, from gnomAD v2.1 exomes. It takes no `dataset` or `reference_genome` and echoes `gnomad_r2_1` and `GRCh37`. A pair gnomAD refuses fails as `cooccurrence_unavailable` with gnomAD's reason; every result carries gnomAD's calibration caveat and its reading of the probability ([#71](https://github.com/cyanheads/gnomad-genetics-mcp-server/issues/71)).
- **Filtering allele frequency**: `gnomad_get_variant` returns `faf95` on the record (`value`, `group`, `source`) and on each `callsets.*` block, and `gnomad_list_gene_variants` rows and its staged table gain `faf95_max` and `faf95_max_group`. `max_faf` keeps rows with a null `faf95_max`, with a notice counting them. A `gnomad_r4` or `gnomad_r3` subset returns a null `faf95` with a notice naming the parent, and `gnomad_variant_triage` step 1 names `faf95` ([#64](https://github.com/cyanheads/gnomad-genetics-mcp-server/issues/64)).
- **HGVS and LOFTEE fields**: `gnomad_get_variant` returns `hgvsc`, `hgvsp`, `lof`, `lof_filter`, `lof_flags`, and `lof_curations` for `transcript_id`, and `primary_transcript` gains `lof_filter` and `lof_flags`. List rows gain `lof` and a `lof_confidence` filter (`HC` or `LC`); `annotations: true` adds `hgvsc`, `hgvsp`, `lof_filter`, `lof_flags`, and `rsids` to rows and the staged table ([#65](https://github.com/cyanheads/gnomad-genetics-mcp-server/issues/65)).
- **Subset datasets**: `dataset` and `GNOMAD_DEFAULT_DATASET` accept gnomAD's ten subsets (`gnomad_r4_non_ukb`, five `gnomad_r3_*`, four `gnomad_r2_1_*`). Coverage and constraint read the parent release, since gnomAD publishes neither for a subset; `gnomad_get_coverage` returns `coverage_dataset` and a notice ([#68](https://github.com/cyanheads/gnomad-genetics-mcp-server/issues/68)).
- **`gnomad_get_gene_constraint` `oe_lof_percentile`, `oe_mis_lower`, `oe_mis_upper`, `oe_syn_lower`, `oe_syn_upper`**: the percentile is null outside gnomAD v4.1.2 and all five are null on `exac` ([#69](https://github.com/cyanheads/gnomad-genetics-mcp-server/issues/69)).
- **`gnomad_get_gene_constraint` `mitochondrial_constraint`**: a mitochondrial gene returns gnomAD's constraint tagged `kind: "protein"` or `"rna"`; null on nuclear genes ([#73](https://github.com/cyanheads/gnomad-genetics-mcp-server/issues/73)).
- **`gnomad_list_gene_variants` `hemizygote_count`**: a nullable row and staged-table column, read from the same source as `ac` and `homozygote_count` ([#75](https://github.com/cyanheads/gnomad-genetics-mcp-server/issues/75)).
- **`gnomad_search_clinvar` somatic classifications**: rows and the `clinvar_variants` table gain `oncogenicity_*` and `clinical_impact_*` fields (classification, review status, last evaluated, conditions), rendered as their own lines in `content[]`. `clinical_significance` and its filter stay germline ([#76](https://github.com/cyanheads/gnomad-genetics-mcp-server/issues/76)).

## Changed

- **`gnomad_search_clinvar` `grch38_variant_id`**: null IDs on deletions, insertions, and duplications are filled from gnomAD's ClinVar mapping for the gene; a failure, a spent deadline, or an unknown gene leaves them null with a notice. Most filled indel IDs return `variant_not_found` from `gnomad_get_variant` ([#66](https://github.com/cyanheads/gnomad-genetics-mcp-server/issues/66)).
- **`gnomad_get_gene_constraint` `content[]`**: prints every constraint value as `structuredContent` holds it; pLI, the o/e values, LOEUF bounds, and Z-scores were rounded to 4 decimals and observed/expected counts to 1 ([#69](https://github.com/cyanheads/gnomad-genetics-mcp-server/issues/69)).
- **`gnomad_search_clinvar` `min_review_stars`**: the `limit` and `min_review_stars` descriptions now say a star-floor window can hold fewer rows than `limit` ([#76](https://github.com/cyanheads/gnomad-genetics-mcp-server/issues/76)).
